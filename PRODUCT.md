# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People using Nuvo on a phone to compete with their crew around real-life goals. Arena is a quick-glance surface: users should immediately see which race needs attention and what action moves the leaderboard.

## Product Purpose

Nuvo turns real-life goals into leaderboard races with a crew. The core loop is to set a finish line, pull in a crew, submit proof, and move the leaderboard.

## Brand Personality

Competitive, direct, and credible. The product should feel energetic without becoming loud, decorative, or game-like at the expense of clarity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard layouts, dense grids of active races, decorative sports graphics, heavy shadows, dead controls, and interfaces that look generated from a marketing template. Never use fake verification or unsupported product promises.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next move first.
2. Make leaderboard progress legible at a glance.
3. Keep one primary action per screen.
4. Use familiar mobile controls and Nuvo product language.
5. Preserve trust through real proof states and working interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, support Dynamic Type without clipping, provide semantic labels for icon controls, keep touch targets at least 44 logical pixels, and avoid requiring color alone to communicate state.

The detailed source of truth remains `docs/NUVO_PRODUCT_MODEL.md`. Stable areas and no-touch boundaries remain defined by `docs/BASELINE_BEFORE_PRODUCT_SKELETON.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
